Project Hail Mary was honestly stunning. The acting, plot, CGI, visuals, and the attention to detail in the biology and aerospace elements all came together beautifully, I wouldn't change a thing. What really sets it apart is how it captures your emotions while simultaneously sparking genuine curiosity, it actually made me interested in aerospace engineering and biology, two fields I'd never have considered before watching it. It's just such a mentally stimulating film, the kind that stays with you and leaves you with so many questions while the film is rolling whether about the plot, the science, physics, the nature of alien life, or something else entirely.

Replace the frontmatter above with:
- **title**: The movie title
- **year**: Release year
- **rating**: Your rating from 1-5 (supports decimals like 4.5)
- **poster**: TMDB poster URL (get from https://www.themoviedb.org)

To get a poster URL from TMDB:
1. Search for the movie on themoviedb.org
2. Click the poster image
3. Right-click and copy image address
4. Or construct it: `https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w300/{poster_path}`
